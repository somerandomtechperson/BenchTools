This is a physics-informed prototype, not a validated digital twin. Estimated top speed uses motor KV, nominal pack voltage, tire circumference, gearing and a load factor. Current draw, voltage sag, temperature, runtime, grip and handling are simulated estimates. Real results depend on motor curves, battery condition, ESC settings, tires, suspension geometry and terrain.
